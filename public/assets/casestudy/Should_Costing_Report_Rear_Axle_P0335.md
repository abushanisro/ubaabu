## Considerations, Assumptions & Exclusions

### Considerations
- Machine and labour hour rates
- Machining allowances
- Gauge inspection
- Merritt standard formats used for cost reports

### Assumptions

| Cost Element | Rate / Basis |
|---|---|
| Amortized Tooling Cost | Included |
| Inbound Logistics of RM | 2% of RM |
| Inventory Cost | 5% of RM |
| Material / Part Handling | 3% of RM |
| Labour Overhead (LOH) | 5% of Labour Cost |
| Logistics | 5% of Inbound Logistics |
| Machine Overhead (MOH) | 8% of Machine Cost |
| Rejection / Misc. Cost | 2% of RM + VA |
| R&D | 2% of DMC + LOH + MOH |
| SG&A | 1% of R&D + DMC + LOH + MOH |
| Profit | 7% of SG&A + R&D + DMC + LOH + MOH |
| Outbound Logistics OH (OLOH) | 2% of Manufacturing Cost |

### Exclusions
- Packaging and shipping cost
- LH & RH brake assembly and brake drum costing
- Painting of the axle assembly
- Cost of lubricants

### Abbreviations

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| RM | Raw Material |
| VA | Value Added (Process cost) |
| DMC | Direct Manufactured Cost |
| LOH | Inbound Logistics Over Heads |
| MOH | Manufacturing Over Heads |
| SG&A | Selling, General and Administrative expenses |
| OLOH | Outbound Logistics Over Heads |
| BOI | Bought Out Item |
