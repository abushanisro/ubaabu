Search architecture · Emithran vs aPriori

Emithran Ranking Engine

A BERT-based content system to outrank apriori.com on should-cost, BOM, and sourcing searches. Google runs BERT to understand pages. We run the same family of models to measure what the top pages cover, find the gaps, and refuse to publish anything thinner than the page we are trying to beat.

As of 2026-11-07Data: Semrush US exports, June 2026

01Summary

aPriori looks big but its organic footprint is narrow. Most of its traffic sits on one off-topic blog post, and none of its top pages are aimed at buyers. Emithran can take #1 on a defined set of non-brand buyer keywords in 6 to 9 months by fixing its own site first, then publishing pages that pass a BERT coverage gate.

What BERT can and cannot do here

Google confirmed in 2019 that BERT helps it understand about 1 in 10 US English searches, and Google's Danny Sullivan said there is nothing to optimize for with BERT specifically. So "using BERT to rank" means using BERT-family models as our measuring instrument. Rankings still come from relevance, quality, and links.

04BERT architecture, and how we use it

BERT reads a whole passage at once and gives every token a vector shaped by the words on both sides. We pool those vectors into one vector per passage, then compare our draft with the pages ranking in the top 10.

Google's side

BERT inside Search

  • Reads the query in context, so "for", "to", and "no" change meaning.
  • Matches the query against passages on your page.
  • Rewards pages that answer the actual question in natural language.
  • You cannot tune for it directly.
Our side

BERT as an instrument

  • Finds the subtopics every top-10 page covers.
  • Scores our draft's coverage before it ships.
  • Checks the draft matches the SERP's intent.
  • Blocks two Emithran pages from competing for one keyword.

07Low-level design

Core scoring code

from sentence_transformers import SentenceTransformer, util

model = SentenceTransformer("sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2")

def embed(texts):
    return model.encode(texts, normalize_embeddings=True, batch_size=32)

def coverage(draft_chunks, subtopics, threshold=0.55):
    """Share of SERP subtopics the draft covers, plus the ones it misses."""
    sims = util.cos_sim(embed(subtopics), embed(draft_chunks))   # subtopics x chunks
    best = sims.max(dim=1).values
    gaps = [t for t, s in zip(subtopics, best) if s < threshold]
    return 1 - len(gaps) / len(subtopics), gaps

def overlap(draft_text, our_pages):
    """Highest similarity to any page we already have (cannibalization check)."""
    d = embed([draft_text])
    p = embed([pg.text for pg in our_pages])
    return float(util.cos_sim(d, p).max())

def gate(draft_chunks, draft_text, subtopics, our_pages, serp_intent, draft_intent):
    cov, gaps = coverage(draft_chunks, subtopics)
    ok = cov >= 0.80 and overlap(draft_text, our_pages) < 0.85 and draft_intent == serp_intent
    return ok, cov, gaps

The 0.55, 0.80, and 0.85 values are starting points. Calibrate them on your first 10 published pages against what actually ranks. A high score means the page is not thin; it does not guarantee a ranking.

Earn links

Copy what worked for aPriori

  • One original cost benchmark (aerospace or defense), like its capex post with 29 domains.
  • A should-cost guide with worked examples, plus a PDF version.
  • Named customer case studies, like its Eaton post with 19 domains.
  • Comparison pages vs aPriori and DFMA; dfma.com already does this.
Get listed

Profiles and associations

  • G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, Gartner Peer Insights, SourceForge, AlternativeTo.
  • Contributed articles for ICEAA, NDIA, AIAA, SME.org.
  • Pitches to IndustryWeek and Tech-Clarity.
  • Wikidata entry and Organization schema with sameAs links.
Show up in AI answers

Channels AI engines cite

  • Short YouTube explainers for each pillar topic.
  • Engineers answering real questions on Reddit, not promoting.
  • LinkedIn and Medium versions of each pillar post, linking back.
  • No self-written Wikipedia article; it gets removed.
